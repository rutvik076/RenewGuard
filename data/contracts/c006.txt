SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Software Subscription Agreement is made with effect from 1 April 2024 (the "Effective Date") between Brightlane Analytics Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales ("Provider"), and Fennimore Retail Group Ltd. ("Customer").

1. SERVICES
1.1 Provider shall make its Insight Dashboard reporting platform (the "Services") available to Customer's authorised users for the duration of this Agreement.
1.2 Provider shall use reasonable endeavours to maintain monthly availability of 99.5%, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least two working days in advance.
1.3 Customer is responsible for the acts and omissions of its authorised users.

2. TERM
2.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and, unless extended under Clause 3, continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months, expiring on 31 March 2026 (the "Initial Term").

3. RENEWAL
3.1 On expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months each (each a "Renewal Term"), unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.
3.2 Notice under this Clause may be given by email to the legal contact address shown on the signature page and takes effect on the day of sending.

4. FEES
4.1 Customer shall pay an annual subscription fee of USD 48,000, invoiced annually in advance and payable within thirty days of the invoice date.
4.2 On the first day of each Renewal Term, the fees shall increase by four percent (4%) over the fees payable for the immediately preceding twelve-month period.
4.3 Overdue sums accrue interest at 2% per annum above the Bank of England base rate.

5. TERMINATION
5.1 Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience during the Initial Term or any Renewal Term.
5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that remains unremedied after a reasonable written request to remedy it.
5.3 On termination, Provider shall make Customer's data available for export for a reasonable period.

6. DATA PROTECTION
6.1 Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law. Provider shall process Customer personal data only on Customer's documented instructions and shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures.

7. LIABILITY
7.1 Neither party excludes liability for death, personal injury caused by negligence, or fraud.
7.2 Subject to Clause 7.1, each party's total aggregate liability in any contract year is limited to the fees paid in that year.

8. GOVERNING LAW
8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction.
