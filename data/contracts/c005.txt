Copperleaf Payroll Technologies Pty Ltd
SUBSCRIPTION ORDER FORM AND TERMS

ORDER SUMMARY
Vendor: Copperleaf Payroll Technologies Pty Ltd
Customer: Bellwether Civil Engineering Pty Ltd
Product: CopperPay payroll and timesheet suite
Order Date / Effective Date: 1 November 2024
Subscription Period: 24 months (1 November 2024 to 31 October 2026)
Subscription Fee: AUD 1,150 per month, billed monthly in arrears

TERMS

1. Term. The subscription starts on the Effective Date and runs for the Subscription Period stated above, ending on 31 October 2026.

2. Auto-Renewal. This subscription will renew automatically at the end of the Subscription Period for a further term of 12 months, and again at the end of each further term, unless it is cancelled in accordance with clause 3.

3. Cancelling Renewal. To cancel automatic renewal, the Customer or Copperleaf must give the other written notice at least 30 days before the end of the current term. Notice given later than this takes effect at the end of the following term.

4. No Early Exit for Convenience. This subscription is non-cancellable during the Subscription Period and during each renewal term. Neither party may end it early for convenience. Clause 5 sets out the only circumstances in which it may end early.

5. Termination for Cause. Either party may end this subscription immediately by written notice if the other party becomes insolvent or commits a serious breach that is not fixed within 21 days of written notice.

6. Support. Copperleaf will provide email and phone support from 8:00am to 6:00pm AEST on business days.

7. Data. Copperleaf will retain Customer payroll data for seven years in line with applicable record-keeping rules and will provide a copy on request.

8. Governing Law. This order is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia.
